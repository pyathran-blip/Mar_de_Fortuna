SEA OF FORTUNE v4 — Fishing game (mouse; touchscreen and gamepad also supported)
==============================================================================

HOW TO PLAY
- Double-click index.html (or JUGAR.bat on Windows). It's a single file: music, logo,
  boat and coin are embedded. No internet needed. Recommended: up-to-date Chrome, Edge
  or Firefox.
- Language: Español / English / Português — on the start screen or in "Display". Saved.

MOUSE CONTROLS (primary)
- Left click on the water: cast the rod (or drop a trap). Move the mouse to guide the hook.
  Click again: reel in.
- When a fish bites: HOLD the left button to reel and LET GO when the tension nears red.
- Hold and drag (on the boat or the water): move the boat.
- Middle click (wheel): jump into the water / back to the boat.
- Underwater the diver follows the mouse. Left click: fire the harpoon.
- Keyboard (optional): P or Esc = pause, M = sound.
TOUCH: tap to cast/reel, hold and drag to move the boat or pull the line, two-finger tap
(or the round 🤿 button) to dive/board; underwater the diver follows your finger.
GAMEPAD: left stick = boat (on the boat) / hook / diver, right stick = cursor;
A = cast/reel (hold = pull), B = dive/board, Start = pause.

THE GAME
- 15 levels with their own scenery, species and sales goal; 28 species; traps (from
  level 2); sharks (a red "!" means a chase — near the surface you're safe); chests;
  aquarium; weather and day/night; 25 medals; island map; upgrade shop (bought on the boat).
- Level 15: buy the Harpoon, dive to the lair and shoot the Kraken's head (3 phases).
- Flopin, a monk parakeet, is always with Lorenzo the fisherman: on his shoulder or the
  boat rail. He talks (speech bubble + voice), warns about sharks, rare bites, chests and
  low oxygen, and with the "Flopin" upgrade he brings you small fish or coins.

SAVES: automatic, 3 slots (name, level, difficulty, playtime). Create / play / delete
(with confirmation) / export / import per slot. An old save is moved to slot 1.
DIFFICULTY: Easy / Normal / Hard — affects sales goals, shark aggression and damage,
oxygen use and shop prices.

ENDING: after beating the Kraken: animated sunset sequence, an illustrated epilogue and
THE END screen with your stats and credits. Then "Keep playing (free mode)" or
"New Game+" (harder; you keep medals, collection, aquarium and boat look).

SOUND: the game's MP3 theme near the surface and in early zones; original WebAudio
ambient tracks in the deep, arctic, volcanic, abyss and Kraken zones (smooth crossfades);
synthesized waves, splashes, seagulls, whale song, bubbles, rain and thunder.
Separate MUSIC and EFFECTS sliders.

Audio starts with your first click, key or touch (browser rule) and is resumed
automatically if the browser suspends it. Flopin speaks with the system voice and also
chirps like a parakeet (audible even if no voices are installed).

DISPLAY: fixed 960x640 canvas scaled by the browser (fast on modest PCs). "Display"
button: effects & particles Low / Normal / High, UI size, screen mode, FPS counter, weather effects, screen shake, colorblind
mode, high contrast, brightness, reduce motion.
Fonts: Baloo 2 and Nunito (SIL Open Font License 1.1).
